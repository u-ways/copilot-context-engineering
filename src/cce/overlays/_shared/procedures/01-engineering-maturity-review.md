description: "Assess or raise a team's engineering maturity: work through the engineering quality-checks index, run a quality review, drive continuous-improvement cycles and record technical debt."

Use this procedure when a team wants to understand where it stands against the framework, or to plan its next step up. Start from `quality-checks.md`, which indexes the checks by area, then run the review as set out in `insights/review.md` and read the outcome alongside the measures in `insights/metrics.md`. Turn findings into a cycle of small improvements using `continuous-improvement.md`, and log anything you decide not to fix now as technical debt following `tech-debt.md`. `principles.md` is the yardstick every check ultimately traces back to.
