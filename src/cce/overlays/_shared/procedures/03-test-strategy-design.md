Use this procedure to plan the test approach for a new service or to review an existing one. `practices/testing.md` is the main reference for test levels and automation; `practices/performance-testing.md` adds load testing and APDEX-style measurement; `practices/structured-code.md` and `tools/sonarqube.md` cover the static checks and quality gates that sit alongside the tests. `patterns/fast-feedback.md` explains why the order and speed of those checks matter as much as their content.
